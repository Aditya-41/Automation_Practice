Notes for Ele_Action.py:
- Sets up Appium driver for Android using UiAutomator2.
- Connects to an emulator with deviceName 'pixel6a' and udid 'emulator-5554'.
- Launches the app 'com.code2lead.kwad' and its main activity.
- Finds a button by content-desc 'Btn1', prints its properties, and clicks it.
- After clicking, finds an EditText by resource-id, sends text "Aditya Patil", and prints its properties.
- Presses the Home key and quits the driver.
- Some commented code for starting/stopping AppiumService.
