* Appium *

1> Reset Strategy
    **<Default>**
    a> Android 
        Stop and clear app data after test . Do not uninstall APK
    b> IOS 
        Shut down sim after test. Do not destroy sim
        If it is a Simulator and app capability is provided , uninstall the 
        app-under-test but does not destroy simulator. If it is a real device or 
        simulator with only "bundleId" capability, does not uninstall app-under-test.

    **"fullReset"**
    a> Android 
        Stop app, clear app data and uninstall apk before session starts and after test

    b> IOS
        Uninstall app before and after real device test, destroy simulator before and after sim
        This happens only before if " resetOnSessionStartOnly : true " is provided


    **"noReset"**
    a> Android
        Do not stop app, do not clear app data, and do not uninstall apk
    
    b> IOS 
        Do not destroy or shut down sim after test. Start test running on Whichever
        sim running or device is plugged in
